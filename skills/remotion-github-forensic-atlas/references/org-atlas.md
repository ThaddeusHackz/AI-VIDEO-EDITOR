## 8. Signals worth acting on

1. **`html-in-canvas` at ★197** is the sleeper: it makes *authentic UI capture* a first-class input. For product demo videos, capture the real app instead of rebuilding it in JSX.
2. **Three SDKs for Lambda** (Ruby/Go/PHP) mean client-side delivery surfaces exist beyond JS — relevant if a client stack is not Node.
3. **`recorder` + `canvas-capture` + `webcodecs-benchmark`** together define the "capture and encode in-browser" lane, which is the cheapest alternative to a render farm for short clips.
4. **`template-overlay` ★19** is the official answer to "I want Remotion output inside Premiere/Resolve": render **overlays with alpha** and composite in the NLE. This repo's NLE positioning should default to that for clients who will not leave their NLE.
5. **`github-unwrapped` at 1,329★** proves the data→video category is the org's most-starred template — data-driven campaign videos are a proven growth surface for a repo like this one.
